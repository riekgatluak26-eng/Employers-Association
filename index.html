OUR FOUNDING AIM

01 · Who We Are

An association built by
employers, for employers.

The Employers Association of South Sudan comprises businesses operating within South Sudan. Our aim is to voice labour and social issues in South Sudan's business environment.

Est. 2012

Over a decade of employer representation

OUR MANDATE

Representing the employer perspective where it matters.

Employers in South Sudan operate in one of the most dynamic environments in the region. EASS exists so that their experience — on hiring, productivity, compliance and growth — is heard where it matters:

In the drafting of labour law and workplace regulation

In the design of social policy and social dialogue

In the national and international conversation about the future of work

HOW WE WORK

02 · What We Do

Four commitments that
shape our work.

From the drafting table of national labour policy to the floor of the International Labour Conference, our work is continuous, practical and member-driven.

01

Labour & social policy

We represent employers' interests in the development of labour legislation, workplace regulation and social policy — ensuring the employer perspective is present from the first draft to the final signature.

02

Social dialogue

We sit at the table with government and workers' organisations, promoting constructive tripartite engagement as the most durable way to resolve the questions that shape the world of work.

03

Regional & international

Through the Confederation of IGAD Employers and the International Labour Organization, we carry South Sudan's business voice into regional and global conversations — from free movement of persons to transhumance.

04

Member services

Information, representation and a network of enterprises that share common interests — a practical platform for employers who want their concerns understood and addressed.

03 · Recent Engagements

Where we've been,
and who we've met.

A record of the association's presence in national, regional and international forums.

JUNE 2025 GENEVA

International Labour Conference — Geneva, Switzerland

Our Chairperson, Prof. Bol Wieu Riak, joined the opening session of the International Labour Organization Conference in Geneva — carrying South Sudan's employers' perspective into the global conversation on the world of work.

APRIL 2025 MOGADISHU

Federal Republic of Somalia — Ministry of Labour & Social Affairs

The Minister of Labour and Social Affairs of the Federal Government of Somalia joined the third meeting of the advisory group on labour and social affairs — part of an ongoing exchange across the Horn of Africa.

FEBRUARY 2025 IGAD

Free movement of persons & transhumance in the IGAD region

Our Chairperson — in his capacity as President of the Confederation of IGAD Employers (CIE) — set out what free movement of persons and transhumance mean for employers across the region.

NOVEMBER 2024 JUBA

ILO Country Office delegation received in Juba

The association hosted Ms. Khumbula Ndaba, Director of the ILO Country Office for Ethiopia, Djibouti, Somalia, Sudan and South Sudan, at its offices in Juba.

JUNE 2024 GENEVA

112th International Labour Conference — Geneva, Switzerland

Our delegation took part in the 112th session of the International Labour Conference, adding the employers' voice from South Sudan to the ILO's standard-setting work.
